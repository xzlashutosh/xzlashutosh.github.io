EMBEDDED SYSTEMS · PRODUCT INTEGRATION

Designing for the complete system

A modular product is only useful when hardware, firmware, and the operator’s workflow fit together.

At Tundra Drone, my work spans electronics and embedded firmware for a modular payload platform. Tundra publicly describes a BASE unit that stays on the drone while compatible modules are attached for different tasks.

My role covers product development, integration, testing, and production readiness. Working across those stages has reinforced the value of validating hardware and software in context, rather than treating each board or firmware feature as an isolated piece.
