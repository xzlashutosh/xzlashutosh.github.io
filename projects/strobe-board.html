Case study / PCB REDESIGN

Strobe
board.

A series of PCB revisions for a compact anti-collision strobe in visible, infrared, and mission-specific configurations.

ROLE: PCB REDESIGNDATE: MAR — AUG 2026TOOLS: KICAD · DFM

At a glance

I updated the strobe PCB across several revisions, resolving component height, connector, electrical, and fabrication issues as the board moved toward release.

PCB layoutPower LEDCurrent limitingPanelizationFabrication release

Board revisions

I relocated tall components to improve the layout, consolidated two connectors into one, and cleared design rule errors. I set the current limiting and selected the high power LED and its current setting resistor.

Fabrication release

I panelized the layout, generated fabrication data, and resolved feedback from the fabrication process.

Outcome

The revised board reached a panelized fabrication release.
