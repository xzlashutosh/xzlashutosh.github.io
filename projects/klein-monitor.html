Case study / EMBEDDED HARDWARE

Klein physiological
monitor.

A wearable monitoring device combining a custom PCB, microcontroller peripherals, and a clear readout of vital measurements.

ROLE: PCB · EMBEDDED FIRMWAREDATE: JAN — MAY 2021TYPE: UNDERGRADUATE PROJECT

At a glance

Klein monitors blood oxygen saturation (SpO₂), ECG waveform, body temperature, and heart rate. It also displays the current date and time alongside the vital parameters.

PCB designMicrocontrollerADCInterruptsEmbedded C

System design

I developed the device PCB and used the microcontroller’s analog-to-digital conversion, interrupts, and scheduling features to acquire and present the measurements. The display combined physiological values with date and time information.
