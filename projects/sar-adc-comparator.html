Case study / ANALOG IC DESIGN

A comparator for
medical ultrasound.

Master’s thesis work designing and optimizing a comparator for a successive-approximation ADC in a 3D medical ultrasound system.

ROLE: MASTER’S THESISDATE: FEB — JUN 2025TOOLS: CADENCE · UMC 180 NM PDK

At a glance

The thesis focused on comparator design for a SAR ADC used in 3D medical ultrasound. I worked with Cadence tools and the UMC 180 nm process design kit, gaining experience in analog circuit design and evaluation.

Analog ICSAR ADCCadenceUMC 180 nmMedical ultrasound

Design context

A comparator is a key decision element in a successive-approximation converter. Its circuit behavior affects conversion speed, power, and the ability to distinguish small input changes. The design needed to be evaluated within the constraints of its process and system context.

Design & analysis

I designed and optimized a comparator circuit in the Cadence design environment, using the UMC 180 nm PDK. The work gave me hands-on practice with analog design flows and the considerations involved in evaluating circuit behavior against system needs.

What I learned

This thesis deepened my experience with analog IC design and Cadence tools. It also strengthened my interest in circuit-level decisions that contribute to the performance of a larger sensing system.
