Case study / ELECTRONICS & PCB

FPV
Light.

Complete PCB design for a visible and infrared illumination module intended for integration into third party drones.

ROLE: COMPLETE PCB DESIGNDATE: SEP 2026SCOPE: SCHEMATIC · LAYOUT · BOM

At a glance

I completed the electronic design and production package for a compact two channel lighting module, covering the LED driver and LED boards.

KiCadLED driverPWM dimmingPCB layoutDFM

Driver architecture

I selected the LED driver and LEDs for approximately 1.5 A per channel with PWM dimming. The design supports visible and infrared channels with switching between them.

Schematic and protection

The schematic includes a low dropout regulator and reverse polarity, transient voltage, electrostatic discharge, and fuse protection. I considered thermal and transient behavior as part of the circuit design.

Layout and manufacturing

I completed the board layout and design rule checks, working through manufacturing limits for aluminum substrate boards and choosing the interconnect approach. I prepared the production data and cost analysis, and checked country of origin for each item in the bill of materials.

Outcome

I completed the circuit, board layout, bill of materials, and fabrication release materials for the lighting module.
