The platform

Tundra Drone publicly describes a modular payload system built around a BASE unit, with interchangeable modules that can be attached to supported drones. Its published lineup includes DROP, SPOTLIGHT, LASER, SPEAKER, STROBE, IR LIGHT, FLOODLIGHT, and BATTERY.

My contribution

I contribute to electronics and embedded firmware for the platform, including product development, integration, testing, and preparation for production. My role has involved working across circuit boards, control and sensing, communications, debugging, and release support.

Engineering approach

I work across hardware and software boundaries, validating designs in the context of the complete system and supporting them through integration and manufacturing.

This overview focuses on the public product context and a high-level summary of my role.
