Case study / MEMS & MIXED SIGNAL

MEMS
accelerometer.

A smart-systems project connecting mechanical device behavior, analog sensing, and mixed-signal simulation.

ROLE: DESIGN & SIMULATIONDATE: FEB — DEC 2024TOOLS: COMSOL · ANSYS · CADENCE

At a glance

I designed and analyzed an accelerometer concept through mechanical simulation and an analog readout path. The work compared simulation results with analytical expectations and explored how the physical response could be translated for digital processing.

MEMSCOMSOL MultiphysicsANSYS WorkbenchCadence VirtuosoAMS 0.65 μm

Mechanical modeling

I used COMSOL and ANSYS Workbench to study the spring constant and resonant frequency, then compared the simulated behavior with analytical results. That comparison helped connect design assumptions with the modeled mechanical response.

Analog readout

I designed an analog readout circuit in Cadence Virtuoso using an AMS 0.65 μm PDK. A voltage-controlled oscillator was used to convert the sensed signal into a form suitable for digital processing.

Mixed-signal evaluation

I built a mixed-signal test environment in ANSYS Twin Builder to evaluate the ROM model alongside the analog and digital circuit components. The approach helped bring device, circuit, and system-level behavior into the same simulation workflow.

Reflection
