Case study / POSITION SENSING

Inductive
position sensing.

A position feedback direction for the gimbal axes, selected to avoid placing a magnet near the drone compass while working toward the pointing accuracy target.

ROLE: SENSOR SELECTION · COIL · PCBDATE: AUG — SEP 2026APPLICATION: GIMBAL AXES

At a glance

I led the selection of a position sensing technology for the two gimbal axes and developed an inductive sensor implementation for integration with the gimbal controller.

Inductive sensingCoil designSimulationPCB designMechanical integration

The sensing constraint

Magnetic encoder feedback presented a risk of interference with the drone compass. The sensor also needed to fit the gimbal's air gap and target geometry while meeting the accuracy goal.

Selection and design

I compared encoder technologies, designed transmitter and receiver coils, and simulated air gap and target material against the accuracy target. I selected a sensing configuration that fit within the available air gap and designed a sensor PCB with the sensing IC and coil structure.

Integration

I planned the connector and mechanical integration into the gimbal controller. The work connected the sensor's electrical design to the physical assembly and the controller's feedback interfaces.

Status

This case study describes the design and integration planning completed through October 2026. It does not claim a completed accuracy validation.
