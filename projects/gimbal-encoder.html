Case study / SENSORS & CONTROL

Gimbal sensing
& stabilization.

Developing the feedback, telemetry, and control that lets a two-axis gimbal keep a payload aligned with the host drone’s camera.

ROLE: CONTROL · SENSORS · PCBDATE: DEC 2025 — SEP 2026TOOLS: SIMPLEBGC · STM32 · RP2040

At a glance

The gimbal is a two-axis stabilizer intended to follow the drone camera’s crosshair. My work covered controller bring-up, quaternion commands, sensor and encoder integration, stabilization tuning, and the design of a new controller board.

Quaternion controlPID tuningAS5048A SPICAN · UARTKiCad

Control & telemetry

I established bidirectional UART between a Raspberry Pi Pico and the BaseCam / SimpleBGC controller, verified motor commands, and developed quaternion control to avoid the singularities that can affect Euler-angle representations.

I integrated drone attitude, heading, and acceleration data into the control path. Bench tests and heartbeat tracking helped isolate packet loss and data-path errors between the host and payload.

Encoder integration

I evaluated SPI magnetic encoders for feedback, fixed the bus mode on the test rig, verified raw position readings through the mechanical rotation, and integrated encoder interfaces into the controller board.

Because magnetic interference could affect the host drone’s compass, I also compared alternative position sensors. The work included inductive sensing evaluation and coil design for a compact board.

Stabilization & board design

Tuning involved follow mode, PID gains, gyro trust, filtering, calibration, and vibration analysis. I used gyro logs to identify resonance and gain behavior that needed further bench investigation.

The controller PCB work covered the system block diagram, hierarchical schematic, power design, IMU and encoder interfaces, CAN, protection, filtering, and connector integration.

Outcome & next steps

The work established a path from gimbal bring-up to encoder-assisted control and a revised controller board. Further validation includes mechanical calibration, vibration testing, and full-system flight evaluation.
