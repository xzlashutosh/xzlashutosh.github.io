About / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROJECTS

Curious by nature.
Practical by design.

Namaste, I’m Ashutosh. I like getting close to the details while keeping sight of the complete system — from the sensor and circuit to the firmware and the person using it.

How I work

Good engineering
happens in the details.

I enjoy the messy middle: the first board bring-up, the signal that does not look right, the mechanical fit that changes the layout, and the test that finally makes the cause clear.

My experience spans PCB design, bare-metal firmware, sensors, control, analog design, and production. I’m comfortable moving between a schematic, code, a scope trace, and a conversation with the people building the hardware.

Before working on airborne systems, I completed a joint international master’s in Smart Systems Integrated Solutions across Norway, Finland, and Hungary. My earlier research explored MEMS, analog circuits, and sensor technology.
